"Sir, your mission objectives are ready for review" - JARVIS

✨ Features Showcase

  • Arc Reactor Animation: Pulsing blue glow in the top-right corner
  • Scanning Effects: Animated scan line moving across the card
  • Progress Tracking: Real-time animated progress bar with shimmer effect
  • Interactive Chores: Click items to mark complete/incomplete
  • JARVIS Messages: Context-aware motivational dialogue
  • Iron Man Theme: Gold and red color scheme with futuristic styling

🎮 Try It Out

Click on any chore item to toggle its completion status and see the animations in action!

Watch how:

  • The progress bar animates smoothly when you complete tasks
  • Completed items get a scanning effect overlay
  • JARVIS provides different motivational messages based on your progress
  • The checkboxes glow when marked complete

📦 Installation

To use this card in your Home Assistant:

  1. Copy custom_components/chore_tracker to your HA config
  2. Copy www/chore-tracker-card.js to your www folder
  3. Add the resource in Lovelace
  4. Restart Home Assistant
  5. Add the card to your dashboard!

See INSTALLATION.md for detailed instructions.
